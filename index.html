The Colloquium

A republic of letters, reflection, and inquiry. In Aperto Latet

May 2026

The Colloquium is an evolving publication space for essays, civic discourse, philosophy, software and systems architecture, poetry and sonnets, AI-generated art, history, and long-form inquiry.

The Colloquium is still taking shape. Some rooms and collections are already open; others remain visible as part of a larger structure whose halls and collections will continue to expand over time.

Chambers

Civic Discussions

Forum Civitatis

Essays and editorials concerned with constitutional order, institutional power, public finance, and civic life.

History

Tabularium

Essays and historical reflections on political movements and ideology, war, propaganda, institutional memory, civilizational change, and the personalities and events that shaped the modern world.

Philosophy

Speculum Realitatis

Essays on simulation, consciousness, metaphysics, language, perception, and the structure of reality.

Software Instruments

Instrumenta Logica

Essays on software engineering, APIs, digital architecture, tooling, systems design, and the hidden logic beneath modern computing.

Engineered Systems

Mechanicum

Reflections on software, platforms, technical systems, organizational machinery, and engineered failure.

Collections
