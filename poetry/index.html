The Colloquium

Poetry

Carmina

A chamber devoted to reflective verse, literary experimentation, and the enduring relationship between language, rhythm, and thought. The works collected here range from Renaissance-inspired forms to modern poetic meditations on science, philosophy, technology, and human experience.

Sonnets

Soneta Anglica

A collection of English and Shakespearean sonnets shaped by meter, rhyme, and the literary traditions refined during the Renaissance and the sixteenth century.

Lyric Verse

Cantilenae

A collection of poems shaped by cadence and imagery, including the quiet musicality and introspective rhythms associated with poets such as Emily Dickinson.

Literary Experiments

Experimenta Litteraria

Explorations in hidden form, constrained language, poetic puzzles, and other experiments at the boundary between prose and verse.

The Art of Scansion

De Metris et Sono

An introductory essay describing poetic meter, stress patterns, rhyme schemes, enjambment, and the author's custom scansion notation system used to deconstruct sonnets and lyrical poems. Readers unfamiliar with these terms may wish to begin with “The Art of Scansion.”
