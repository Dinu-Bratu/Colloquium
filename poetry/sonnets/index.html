Soneta Anglica

A chamber devoted to English and Shakespearean sonnets shaped by the traditions of meter, rhyme, and formal poetic structure that emerged during the Renaissance and came to prominence in the sixteenth century. These works unfold across fourteen lines of iambic pentameter arranged into three quatrains and a closing couplet, following the traditional ABAB CDCD EFEF GG rhyme scheme.

Many of the works within this collection extend those traditions into modern themes including information theory, artificial intelligence, simulation, entropy, and quantum uncertainty.
