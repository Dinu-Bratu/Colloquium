Soneta Anglica

A chamber devoted to English and Shakespearean sonnets shaped by the traditions of meter, rhyme, and formal poetic structure that emerged during the Renaissance and came to prominence in the sixteenth century. Many of the works within this collection extend those traditions into modern themes including information theory, the imperfection of language, ambiguity, entropy, and quantum uncertainty.
