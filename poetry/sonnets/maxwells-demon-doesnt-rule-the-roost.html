The Colloquium

Maxwell’s Demon Doesn’t Rule the Roost

In dig’tal realms—where ones and zeros reign

Supreme—lost broadcasts, noise, and static rule

The day. A signal dropped is re-obtained

To re-compute a message misconstrued.

Our digital machines precisely stream

ACKnowledgement of messages exchanged—

Small quantum information packets themed

To ‘Yes’ or ‘No.’ But human speech—constrained

By words alone—has no such in-built mode

For validating truth and falsity.

Instead, we string together words imbued

With shades of gray and ambiguity.

Since Maxwell’s Demon doesn’t rule the roost,

We need to speak with more unspoken truths.

Ken Stanfield
